Here's how Octo works.
The quick version.
Create projects, add tasks
Tap + in the sidebar. Set timing — Today, This Week, Someday. Tap + on any task to expand it for subtasks, notes, and due dates.
Start every day on My Day
Weather, a daily prompt, habit rings, and your news feed. Tap rings to log habits. This is your morning screen.
Talk to Claude
Type or speak. "Add X to project Y for today." "Mark Z done." "What should I focus on?" Claude sees your tasks and acts on them.
Share your inbox link
Go to Inbox, create a link, send it to anyone. They send you tasks, lists, or notes. You decide where they go.
